# Developer Check-In

This document captures a brief check-in prompt used to understand who is working on the codebase and what they are focusing on in a given session.

## Check-In Questions

1. **What is your name?**
2. **What are you working on today?**

## Purpose

These questions help orient contributors and collaborators, making it easier to:

- Attribute work to the right person
- Track session goals and priorities
- Facilitate async communication across the team
